# 15-Block Prompt — Letter A (ready for Seedance 2.5)

Copy this block as the base prompt. Only change what the templates tell you to change for future letters.

---

1. **Style:** Bright Pixar-style 3D children’s animation, soft rounded shapes, saturated friendly colors, clean toy-like surfaces, warm cinematic lighting safe for toddlers.

2. **World:** Sunny Alphabet Zoo with soft green hills and colorful letter-shaped animal houses under a clear blue sky with gentle clouds. Warm golden morning light.

3. **Host:** Zippi the Zookeeper — soft rounded anthropomorphic fox, warm orange fur with cream chest and inner ears, large sparkling amber eyes, small black nose, gentle smile, tiny blue explorer hat with yellow star pin, short yellow scarf, simple blue shorts. Cheerful, inviting, slightly silly energy.

4. **Guest:** Avery the Alligator — soft mint-to-leaf green cartoon alligator with pale yellow belly, large round friendly eyes, short rounded snout with no sharp teeth, small rounded limbs, red circular badge on the chest with white letter A. Soft toy-like surface, slightly shy then proud.

5. **Action:** Zippi hops into the sunny zoo and waves. He points to a big green letter-A house. Avery peeks out, then steps forward. Avery does a happy spin and a soft toothless snap. Both turn to camera and sing the letter chant together with clear mouth movement. Soft musical notes float in the air. Zippi waves goodbye as a glowing letter A floats upward.

6. **Camera:** Starts wide with a gentle push-in, moves to a readable medium two-shot, drops to a slightly low angle for the play moment, holds a stable close-up for the chant, then soft pull-back for the outro. Simple, clear, never shaky.

7. **Duration & pacing:** Approximately 24 seconds. Lively but calm enough for toddlers. No frantic motion.

8. **Lighting:** Soft warm golden key light, gentle fill, no hard shadows on faces, high-key and friendly.

9. **Audio:** Upbeat playful children’s chant with light claps, clear sing-along vocals, soft animal accent sounds. Native audio preferred.

10. **Chant / dialogue:** “A is for Avery! Avery the Alligator! A-A-Avery, come and play!”

11. **Emotion:** Joyful, proud, inviting, safe.

12. **Reference roles:** Hold Zippi’s face, fur colors, hat, scarf, and proportions exactly. Hold Avery’s green/yellow colors, round eyes, red A badge, and soft mouth exactly. Do not invent extra characters or change the badge.

13. **Negatives:** No realistic animals, no sharp teeth, no dark or scary lighting, no text on screen, no extra characters, no adult proportions, no muddy colors, no horror elements.

14. **Continuity:** Same art style, same lighting language, same character designs as the rest of the Alphabet Zoo series.

15. **Ending:** Clean soft pull-back, glowing letter A floating up, gentle fade to bright.
