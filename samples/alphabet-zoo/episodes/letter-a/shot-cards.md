# Episode: Letter A — Avery the Alligator

**Target length:** 24 seconds  
**Aspect:** 9:16  
**Primary assets:** `@zippi`, `@avery`, `@zoo_day`

---

### Shot 1 — World + Host (0–4s)
- **Purpose:** Establish the Alphabet Zoo and bring Zippi on screen
- **Framing:** Wide, gentle push-in
- **Action:** Sunny hills and letter-shaped houses. Zippi hops in from the right, waves at camera with an open, happy expression.
- **Camera:** Slow push toward Zippi
- **Audio:** Soft intro music bed begins
- **Notes:** No other characters yet. Keep the frame clean.

### Shot 2 — Introduction (4–9s)
- **Purpose:** Reveal the letter and the animal
- **Framing:** Medium
- **Action:** Zippi points toward a big friendly green letter-A house. Avery peeks out shyly, then steps fully into frame. Zippi turns to camera, excited.
- **Camera:** Slight orbit or gentle reframing to keep both characters readable
- **Audio:** Light accent as Avery appears

### Shot 3 — Play moment (9–16s)
- **Purpose:** Pure cuteness and movement before the chant
- **Framing:** Medium to medium-close, low angle preferred
- **Action:** Avery does a happy little spin and a soft, toothless snap of the jaws. Zippi claps. Soft musical notes begin to float.
- **Camera:** Low tracking or gentle arc
- **Notes:** Keep Avery’s mouth soft — no teeth.

### Shot 4 — Chant (16–22s)
- **Purpose:** Clear sing-along moment with readable lip movement
- **Framing:** Close-up favoring Avery, Zippi visible on the side
- **Action:** Both look toward camera. Avery leads the chant, Zippi joins. Mouths open clearly on the key syllables.
- **Camera:** Stable or very gentle push
- **Audio:** Full chant — see song.md
- **Notes:** This is the most important shot for educational value. Prioritize face readability and lip sync.

### Shot 5 — Outro (22–24s)
- **Purpose:** Clean, loopable ending
- **Framing:** Wider pull-back
- **Action:** Zippi waves goodbye. A soft glowing letter “A” floats upward and sparkles. Gentle fade.
- **Camera:** Soft pull-back
- **Audio:** Chant resolves, music softens
