# Alphabet Zoo — Series Bible

## Logline

Every letter has a friend at the Alphabet Zoo. Zippi the Zookeeper introduces them one by one with a short, catchy chant that toddlers can sing along to.

## Format

- **Length**: 20–28 seconds per short
- **Aspect**: Prefer 9:16 (Shorts / TikTok / YouTube Shorts) or 1:1
- **Structure** (never break this):
  1. Wide world + Zippi entrance
  2. Introduce the letter house / animal
  3. Playful moment
  4. Close-up chant with clear mouth movement
  5. Soft outro + floating letter

## Tone & Safety Rules

- Bright, warm, safe, never scary
- Soft rounded shapes only
- No sharp teeth, no realistic animal anatomy, no dark lighting
- Expressions are always happy, curious, or gently proud
- Zero text burned into the video (captions are added in post if needed)

## Recurring Assets (must stay locked)

| Asset | Tag | Status |
|-------|-----|--------|
| Zippi the Zookeeper | `@zippi` | Locked in this sample |
| Alphabet Zoo world | `@zoo_day` | Defined in style-lock |
| Letter animals | `@avery`, `@bruno`, … | One passport per letter |

## Series Scaling Rule

Once `@zippi` and the world rules are locked, each new letter only requires:

1. One new animal passport
2. Stress-test that passport against Zippi (two-shot)
3. Fill the templates with the new name + chant

Everything else stays identical. That is the entire production system.

## Educational Goal

Each short teaches **one letter** through identity + repetition + song. No secondary lessons in the same clip.
