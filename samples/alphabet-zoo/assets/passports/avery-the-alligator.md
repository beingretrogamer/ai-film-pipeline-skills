# Passport: Avery the Alligator (Letter A)

**Tag:** `@avery`  
**Status:** Locked (sample)  
**Version:** 1.0

## Exhaustive Descriptor (copy verbatim)

Avery is a soft, friendly cartoon alligator in the same bright Pixar-style 3D children’s animation as Zippi. Soft mint-to-leaf green body with a pale yellow belly. Large round friendly eyes with dark pupils and soft eyelids — never slit pupils. Short rounded snout, no visible sharp teeth (mouth is a soft curved line that opens for singing). Small rounded limbs, gentle proportions, slightly chubby. Wears a simple red circular badge on the chest with a clear white letter “A”. Personality reads as a little shy at first, then proud and happy when saying its name. Same soft warm lighting language as the rest of the Alphabet Zoo. No scales that look realistic or hard; surface is soft and toy-like.

## Must stay identical

- Soft green + yellow belly colors
- Large round friendly eyes
- Red “A” badge on chest
- Absence of sharp teeth
- Overall soft rounded silhouette

## Ban list

- Realistic crocodile/alligator texture or teeth
- Scary or aggressive expression
- Dark green or muddy colors
- Missing or wrong letter badge
- Adult or elongated proportions
