## Exhaustive Descriptor (copy verbatim)

Zippi is a soft, rounded anthropomorphic fox character in bright Pixar-style 3D children’s animation. Young-adult energy, warm and inviting. Slightly chubby cheeks, large sparkling amber eyes with visible catchlights, small neat black nose, permanent gentle closed-mouth smile that can open into a clear singing mouth. Warm orange fur with a cream-colored chest and cream inner ears. Soft, clean fur texture — never realistic or wiry. Body proportions are child-friendly and rounded: short limbs, soft belly, no sharp angles. Wears a tiny bright blue explorer hat with a single yellow star pin on the front and a short yellow scarf loosely tied around the neck. Simple blue shorts. No other clothing. Posture is upright and energetic, often leaning slightly forward with open body language. Always appears friendly, slightly silly, and excited to introduce a new animal friend. Lighting on Zippi is always soft warm key with gentle fill; no hard shadows on the face.
