# Global Ban List — Alphabet Zoo

These are non-negotiable. Any generation that violates them is rejected before it reaches selects/.

- Realistic animal anatomy, fur, or scales
- Sharp teeth, claws, or any threatening features
- Dark, stormy, night, or horror lighting
- Scary, sad, angry, or crying expressions on main characters
- Text, logos, or watermarks burned into the frame
- Adult human proportions or clothing styles
- Blood, injury, or distress of any kind
- Extra random characters not in the passport set
- Changing Zippi’s hat, scarf, or fur colors
- Changing a letter animal’s badge or core colors
